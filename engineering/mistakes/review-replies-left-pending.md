# Review Replies Left as Pending

## What Happened

After addressing review feedback on a pull request, I believed I had replied to the reviewer, but the replies had only been added to a **Pending review** and were not visible to others.

As a result, it appeared that I had ignored the review comments for several days.

## Why It Happened

The most likely cause was accidentally starting a review.

Once a review has been started, subsequent replies are added to the pending review until it is explicitly submitted.

Because pending replies are not immediately visible to other reviewers, it is easy to overlook that the replies have not been published.

## Lesson Learnt

When replying to review comments, distinguish between posting a reply immediately and adding a comment to a pending review.

- Understand when GitHub creates a pending review.
- Be aware that comments in a pending review are not visible to other reviewers.
- Confirm that replies have been published before considering the review feedback addressed.

## Prevention

- Avoid starting a review unless you intend to submit one.
- If a review has been started, finish by selecting **Submit review** or **Cancel review**.
- Before leaving the pull request, check for any pending reviews.
- Confirm that important replies are visible to other reviewers.
